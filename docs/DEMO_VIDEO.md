## Suggested 2-minute script

Keep it tight — a judge is deciding whether the prototype is real.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Onboarding | "NIRVANA is for elders with mild cognitive change — non-clinical, no pressure, fully offline." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Home → a game | Show the 64dp targets and supportive wording. Play *Remember Objects* and hit **Hint**. |
| 0:40–1:05 | **Airplane mode ON** | "Watch — I'm turning off the network." Do a reminder and finish a game. Everything still works. |
| 1:05–1:25 | Airplane mode OFF | Show the sync queue drain to the cloud. |
| 1:25–1:50 | Caregiver dashboard | Patient switcher, 7-day participation chart, reminder status. |
| 1:50–2:00 | Close | "Offline-first, non-clinical, and the family stays informed." |

**The airplane-mode segment is the money shot** — it is the one thing a judge
cannot verify by clicking around the web demo.
