## LinkedIn (professional)
Introducing NIRVANA: an offline-first companionship and care-coordination app for elders,
built with Flutter for Smart India Hackathon.

Elders get cognitive-engagement games, on-device reminders that work with the network
unplugged, family-familiar content, and an elderly-friendly "Ask NIRVANA" companion.
Caregivers get a separate dashboard showing participation trends and reminder
acknowledgements — visibility, not surveillance.

Under the hood: Riverpod, GoRouter, Hive as a local-first source of truth, Supabase +
PostgreSQL with role-separated data, and idempotent sync events. The AI runs through a
secure backend/Edge Function, never from a key inside the app.

NIRVANA is a prototype: it supports games and reminders, and does not diagnose or treat
dementia. Non-clinical by design — it never says "failed".
